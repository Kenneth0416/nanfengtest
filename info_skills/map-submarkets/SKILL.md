---
name: map-submarkets
description: Map the market names a publisher prints to the team's submarkets, and note where the publisher's boundary differs.
---
- Call `map_markets(names, metric)` with the names exactly as printed.
- Publishers draw boundaries differently: "City" may mean City Core or City & Southbank; "Docklands"
  usually means Docklands & Stratford; "West End" prime rents are quoted for the West End core
  (Mayfair/St James's). The tool adds a boundary note when the mapping is not exact: keep it.
- A name that maps to nothing is dropped from the figures, and you say which names were dropped.
- Never move a figure to a different submarket to make it match another source.
