# Storyline

A brief deck answers one question for a busy reader: what moved in the London office market, and does
it change what we do? Each slide makes one point; the title states it and the so-what says why it matters.

## Order

Team brief (one deck for the board and the investment committee): Cover · Occupiers (Two charts or Hero
number on `vacancy_pct`) · Title only with `evidence` (the team's submarkets) · Rents (Chart + takeaway on
`rents`) or Capital markets (Chart + table: `investment`, `yields`) · Signal cards (title states the case
for and against) · Sources. Theme `navy-gold`; `data-white` when it is going to the committee on its own.

Short on time or data: Cover · Signal cards · Sources is a valid three-slide deck.

Client or owner update (`forest-cream`, see `examples/showcase-spec.json`): Cover · Statement (the call
in one sentence) · Stat row · Numbered pillars (what it means) · Timeline · Sources.

## Words

- **Eyebrow:** slide number and topic, `02 · Occupiers`. Cover: `London office market · <date or pack>`.
- **Title:** a sentence stating the finding, under 90 characters, sentence case: "Vacancy fell to 8.0%,
  the lowest since Q1 2024". Not a topic label.
- **Statement:** under 60 characters, two short clauses; put the turn in `*asterisks*`.
- **Pillars:** a three-word head and one or two sentences with figures from the data.
- **so_what:** one sentence, the consequence, not a repeat of the title.
- **Labels:** what is plotted and the unit: "Prime rent, £/sq ft, Q2 2026".
- **takeaway:** two to four short lines, each carrying its footnote mark.
- **source:** the footnote and anything missing ("Quarters with no figures are not shown").
- **notes:** two or three sentences an analyst would say out loud, including flagged figures and unconfirmed talk.

## Figures

Write figures and periods exactly as the data gives them. Use a percentage or a change only if the data
carries it (`vs_lta_pct`, `change`). Name a cause only if a signal card or the headline gives it. Market
talk stays in the talk strip, labelled unconfirmed.
