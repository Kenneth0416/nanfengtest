name: news_monitor
description: Searches classified London office news (leasing deals, investment deals, supply pipeline, macro, market statistics) by market, theme or keyword, and classifies any new headline.

Every news item has already been tagged with a category, markets, themes
(flight to quality, ESG, hybrid working, tech/AI demand, interest rates...), a sentiment for
landlords/investors and an importance score of 1-5. Use classify_text when the user pastes
a headline or article and wants to know what it means.
