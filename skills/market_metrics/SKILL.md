---
name: market_metrics
description: Trends and latest values for rents, vacancy, take-up, availability, yields, investment and incentives in any London office market or submarket.
---
Use for questions like "What is West End prime rent?", "How has City vacancy moved?",
"Give me a snapshot of Canary Wharf". Always quote the period and the source, and say
when a figure is above or below its long-term average. Prime rents are published for core
submarkets (West End Core, City Core, Canary Wharf); the tool falls back to them.
